Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Neon Breaker handles information when you use the game.

Neon Breaker is intended to provide an offline, single-player brick-breaking experience. This policy applies to the Neon Breaker mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Neon Breaker is designed not to collect, request, or transmit personal information to the developer.

The game may save gameplay information, such as your score or highest score, locally on your device so that you can track your progress.

This locally stored gameplay information is not sent to the developer.

3. Data Sharing

The game does not share personal information with the developer or third parties, provided the published version contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or other third-party data-collection services.

4. Internet and Permissions

The game is intended to work offline and should not require internet access or unnecessary device permissions.

The final application's actual permissions and included software components determine its behaviour.

5. Data Storage and Deletion

Gameplay records, including a saved high score, may remain on your device until they are reset by the game, if that feature is available, or the application's data is cleared or the application is uninstalled.

We do not maintain a developer-side database of gameplay records.

6. Children's Privacy

Neon Breaker does not intentionally request personal information from players. If the game is made available to children, its design, content, target audience, and distribution must also comply with applicable children's privacy requirements and Google Play policies.

7. Third-Party Services

This policy assumes that the published version does not include advertising networks, analytics tools, tracking SDKs, or other third-party services that collect user data.

If such services are added, this policy must be updated accordingly.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features or data practices change. The updated policy will include a revised date.
